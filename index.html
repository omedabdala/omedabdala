02 ABOUT

I design for the screen, the page and the physical world.

I am Omed Abdala, a multidisciplinary graphic designer working across logos, brand identities, posters, social media, books, editorial layouts, signage and other forms of visual communication.

My experience also extends into print production and visual fabrication. I prepare artwork for wide-format and flex printing, UV printing, CNC routing, laser cutting and engraving, sign-making and related production workflows. This practical knowledge helps me create designs that are visually strong, technically accurate and ready to be produced.
