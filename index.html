About me

I design with intention, precision and depth.

I am Omed Abdala, a multidisciplinary graphic designer creating visual systems that connect brands with people and ideas with meaning.

My practice spans logos, brand identities, posters, social media, books, editorial layouts and signage. I also understand real production workflows from prepress and wide format printing to UV printing, CNC routing, laser cutting and engraving so every concept can move confidently from screen to reality.
